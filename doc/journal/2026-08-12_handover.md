## Key Decisions
- **`persistent` stays the fiducial stochastic layer** — now
  evidence-backed, not just conservative: single-epoch applications are
  PROVABLY rho-blind, and a real survey observes each galaxy once, so
  the change would buy nothing there while costing differential-
  deposition fidelity. `ar1` ships as the cross-epoch option.
- **rho is MEASURED, never tuned to the judged scores** (mirrors exp37's
  treatment), so the layer gains no fitting freedom — it corrects a
  wrong implicit constant (rho = 1) rather than adding a knob.
- Portability, settled with the user: applying the model to gravity-only
  N-body boxes means inheriting theta, the deviation pool AND rho from
  TNG300 (no galaxies there to recalibrate against) — accepted. For
  fitting OBSERVATIONS, rho never enters the likelihood (a survey sees
  each galaxy once); the pool is the piece that does, and it could be a
  fitted nuisance parameter rather than inherited.
- Self-correction recorded: the stage-2 claim that rho=1 over-persistence
  contributes to the high-z PLANE stall was FALSIFIED in stage 3 (those
