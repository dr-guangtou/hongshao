## THE CORRECTED PICTURE — `score_F`, the fixed 840-galaxy z=2 cohort

| bound | freedom/galaxy | mean |
|---|---|---|
| monotone in time, shape loss only | basis-free | **0.009** — *not a floor* |
| monotone in time, amplitude + shape | basis-free | 0.195 |
| monotone in time, absolute-fractional (exact PAVA) | basis-free | 0.286 |
| each epoch fitted alone (not causal) | 5 x ~71 | <= 0.164 |
| **one causal weight vector, EXACT production shape loss** | ~71 | **0.282** |

| *control*: fitted to ANOTHER galaxy, exact loss | ~71 | **0.319** |
| 8 deposit-group amplitudes (surrogate) | 8 | 0.614 |
| **the fitted model** | 0 (7 global) | **0.898** |

Two numbers carry the section.

**The exact ceiling is 0.282, not 0.538.** `score_F` and `score_A^2` are means
over galaxies of per-galaxy terms, so each can be minimised galaxy by galaxy
and the population minimum obtained EXACTLY — `stage34_objective.py` does that
with analytic gradients (checked to 1e-8). The surrogate overstated the bound
by 90 per cent.

**The permuted control is 0.319 under the same objective.** One galaxy's
deposits fitted directly to a DIFFERENT galaxy's measured profile costs only 13
per cent against fitting the right one. **Seven eighths of the 0.282-to-0.898
gap is dictionary overcompleteness, not information a global law could use.**
That is the single most important caution in the experiment and it is now
measured on the right footing.

## WITHDRAWN

1. ~~"Two thirds of the z=2 central deficit is irreducible."~~ Sweeping the z=2
   epoch weight in the basis-free monotone bound moves the z=2 residual at
   2 kpc from **−23.2% to −0.9%**, while the z=0.4 overshoot runs **+4.6% to
   +40.4%** and `score_F` runs 0.209 to 0.477. At the production weighting the
   optimum is **−17.2%**; the model is **−25.4%**. What is structural is the
   trade-off, not the number.
2. ~~"The monotone floor is 0.29 in `score_F`."~~ **0.009.** The shape loss
   renormalises each epoch at 100 kpc while monotonicity constrains the
   ABSOLUTE profile, and any set of shapes can be made monotone by inflating
   the later epochs. Stage 3.0's projection scored 0.29 because it chose to
   match the amplitude.
3. ~~"Zero headroom at z=2."~~ Every pre-z=2 deposit sits in one epoch
   interval, so rescaling its single amplitude cannot change a profile
   normalised at 100 kpc. The rung HAD to equal the model at z=2.
4. ~~"The basis cannot draw the CHANGES between epochs."~~ With the increment
   solve in the units it reports, the truth's own decline costs more than the
   basis at every interval (2.4–4.7% against 1.8–3.2%).
5. ~~"The compact second channel is retired."~~ **Deprioritised.** See below.

## Warnings carried forward (new ones first)

- **A bound must be optimised on the same objects and the same functional it is
  reported on.** Three versions of that error in one afternoon: a solve using
  epochs its score excluded; a test weighted by one quantity and reported in
  another; a surrogate quoted as the production loss (a factor of two).
- **Check whether a constraint binds the quantity you report.** Monotonicity is
  a true theorem that puts essentially no floor under a renormalised shape loss.
- **A control can be an identity.** Ask what it is ABLE to change first.
- **Split on the mechanism before modelling the defect.** One preregistered
  binary split retired a planned experiment more decisively than a fit would.
- **Never let a smoke path write production filenames.** It now writes `_smoke`.
- Earlier: a benchmark and the thing it benchmarks must be scored on the same
  objects; classify what a fix invalidates from what the code READS; import
  `fit` before `halo`/`model` in exp54; an unbounded metric can be dominated by
  one object; `setsid` does not exist on macOS — watch the LOG ADVANCE; long
  runs checkpoint per unit of work; rank by the judge never by loss;
  multi-start is mandatory; `np.interp` CLAMPS; a SLICE is not a PROFILE;
  truncating a profile moves its half-mass radius; usetex eats `%`; matplotlib
  `wrap=True` does not work under `savefig.bbox="tight"`.
- **Scale your variables before handing them to an optimiser.** L-BFGS-B on
  weights of 1e10 with a gradient of 1e-11 reports success at its starting
  point. It happened twice in this experiment (BVLS, then the exact-objective
