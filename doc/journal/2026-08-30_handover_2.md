- The raw-accuracy leader is the six-coordinate free damped cosine,

  ```text
  T(u) = exp(-lambda u) cos(omega u).
  ```

  Its epoch-median full-CoG RMS is 0.00167--0.00178 dex, 13.67--38.37% lower than unrestricted double Sersic's 0.00197--0.00275 dex on the same profiles. Its epoch-median shell-density RMS is 12.40--24.46% lower than double Sersic at every epoch. Expanding the squared term leaves a finite sum of elementary exponential--trigonometric integrals, so `Q(u)` and `Sigma(R)` are closed form.
