## THE FOUR PROPOSALS (the next session's work, in order)

1. **Test the unification** — half a day, NO fitting. Per galaxy, is the
   plane residual predicted by the M*(<5 kpc) bias? exp45 already measured
   Spearman(dlog R50, M(<5) bias) = -0.89 to -0.97 at every epoch. If the
   plane residual follows too, the central-region and high-z problems are
   ONE defect and every later test gets a single target. **Do first.**
3. **Fix the scoring before fixing the model** — cheap, and plausibly why
   past attempts failed. Compact galaxies are 6% of the z=0.4 sample, so
   every previous fix was judged on an average they barely enter; that may
   be why the exp38 core channel and retention floor "halved the inner
   deficit but broke the differential physics". Score compact and extended
   sub-populations separately. **Do second (1 and 3 together define the
   target and the scoring before any fitting).**
2. **Free the efficiency window, judged on compact galaxies** — the
   expensive one; hold until 1 and 3 land. The window is narrow (mu=1.52,
   sigma=0.24 -> peak z~3.6, half-max z~2.6-4.8, most mass inside ~1.2
   Gyr), compressing the range of arrival times so every halo gets a
   near-identical centre. Three pointers: sigma is the ONLY lever on
   cross-epoch coherence (exp45 follow-up 1); exp42's real-MAH refit chose
   sigma=0.52 and gained most at z=1.5/2.0; exp45 flagged that refit as a
   partial coherence exception.
4. **A compactness-aware stochastic width** — only ~14-22% of compactness
   is halo-predictable at fixed mass (linear), so the rest cannot be
   captured by ANY deterministic halo-conditioned model. This is the
   pinned "halo-dependent deviation WIDTH" item, now with a measured
   motivation.

**Do NOT chase** ~1.5 of the mass-size plane's 5.0 at z=2 — it is the
below-grid extrapolation artifact.

## Method warnings for the next session (all measured, not guessed)

  from 3.5 to 4.1, worse than all 24 random one-galaxy removals. Report p
  against random same-n trims, never raw deltas.
