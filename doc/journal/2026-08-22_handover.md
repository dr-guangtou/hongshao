## THE HEADLINE — read this first

The adopted kernel pins `M_model(<500 kpc) == M_TNG(<500 kpc)` **per galaxy,
per epoch** (verified to **2.2e-16**), and its deposit weights are normalized to
sum to 1, so it **carries no absolute stellar mass at all**. 100% of the mass
and 100% of the mass growth come from the pinned value.

Therefore:
- The Mh–M\*(<500) relation is **inserted, not predicted**, with its scatter and
  its evolution. It is not a fit; it is an identity.
- **The stated use case cannot be executed**: an N-body halo has no
  `M_TNG(<500)` to copy — that quantity is what the model is for. Supply an
  external SHMR and *that* relation, not hongshao, assigns the mass.
- The adopted product is an **ORACLE-AMPLITUDE SHAPE MODEL**: *given a halo and
  its already-known stellar mass, predict the radial distribution.* Legitimate
  and useful — but not an Ultimate SHMR.

**Decision (user + independent reviewer, 2026-08-22):** reclassify it under that
honest name, and make removal of truth pinning the first structural goal of the
next experiment. Full plan: `doc/plans/2026-08-22-exp54-unpinned-amplitude.md`.

## Problems / Blockers

- **Every absolute QA number in the program** (exp38, exp40, exp47, exp48,
  exp53) is quoted under the pinned condition and must be **re-baselined**.
  Measured: the tier 2b z=0.4 plane moves **2.1 -> 2.9** under a realistic
  0.137 dex amplitude error, and -> 3.6 at 0.20 dex.
- **The compact-galaxy central deficit is unexplained again** (-39% to -47%
  regardless of transport, family, or window).
- **exp53's fits are IN-SAMPLE** — 10-fold CV over 17 cells x 3 starts was
  unaffordable.
- **The conditioning vector is built from z=0.4 DESCENDANT properties and
  applied at every epoch.** Fine for tracking progenitors of a selected sample;
  impossible for populating an arbitrary z=2 halo catalogue.
- The pinned quantity is **extrapolated** (7.2% of `M(<500)` beyond the last
  measured radius at z=0.4) and its form systematic (0.021 dex) **tilts with
  halo mass by -0.021 dex** — the same size as exp53's decisive signal. Absolute
  tilts carry that systematic; **differences between models do not**, because
  all models share one `m500`.

## Key Decisions

1. **Reclassify the pinned kernel as `oracle_amplitude_shape_model`.** Its
   absolute-mass QA values must never again be quoted as forward-model scores.
2. **The q-grid was scoped OUT of exp53** (user, 2026-08-21) as answering a
   transport-tuning question; the "trim q to 0.6-0.8" recommendation it produced
   is **withdrawn** from exp53's findings and kept only in an appendix.
3. **Normalized representation != truth pinning.** The successor keeps
   normalized shapes internally — `log M*(<R) = A_pred(H) + log F_pred(<R|H)` —
   and removes only the copying of the truth.
4. **The reference aperture must be MEASURED** (100 or 148 kpc), not the
   extrapolated 500.
5. **The objective must score amplitude and shape separately** — a realistic
   0.137 dex amplitude error costs **2.05x the entire current loss**, so a naive
   un-normalized fit would ignore shape. (Note: the RMS does NOT multiply a
   coherent error by 24; it leaves it at full strength while diluting a local
   feature.)
6. **Removing the pin CLOSES the aperture-horizon trap**, it does not open it —
   un-normalized, mass beyond the aperture is simply absent and the fit pays.
   But it does **not** remove every efficiency-outer-tail degeneracy; measure
   that explicitly.
