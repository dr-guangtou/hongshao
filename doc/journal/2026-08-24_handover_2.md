New file `stage34_objective.py` computes the bound under the **exact loss we

Analytic gradients, verified against finite differences to 1e-8.

**The corrected numbers, on the 840 galaxies above the redshift-2 threshold
followed at all five epochs.** Profile-shape error as a typical percentage
error of the normalised cumulative profile (previous generation = 15.8%):

| what is allowed to vary | shape error |
|---|---|
| anything, provided enclosed mass never falls | 0.1% — *not a floor* |
| the same, with the total mass also constrained | 3.1% |
| every deposit's mass, refitted separately at each epoch | 2.6% |
| **every deposit's mass, one set for all five epochs** | **4.5%** |
| *the same, fitted to a DIFFERENT galaxy's profile* | **5.0%** |
| the mass in each of 8 broad time intervals | 9.7% |
| **the fitted model, 7 shared parameters** | **14.2%** |

**Four conclusions withdrawn**: "two thirds of the redshift-2 central deficit
is irreducible"; "the floor is 0.29"; "zero headroom at redshift 2"; "the
deposits cannot draw the changes between epochs".

**Two findings that replaced them.** First, the redshift-2 central deficit is
two failures added: for the **53%** of these galaxies whose measured mass
inside 4.9 kpc later *falls*, the model puts **37% too little** stellar mass
inside 2 kpc at redshift 2 and a perfect efficiency rule would still be 22% too
light; for the other **47%** the model is only **11%** too light and a perfect
rule closes half. Second, the trade-off is structural but the number is not: a
model of this kind can place that residual anywhere from −23% to −1%, at the
cost of a +5% to +40% central overshoot at redshift 0.4.

### Next step 1, in detail

**Replace the single power law in `ln(1+z)` in the efficiency law with
something with two or three more shared parameters** — a low-order polynomial
in `ln(1+z)`, or a two-knot spline — and fit it on all five epochs.

- **Why**: freeing the deposited mass over K broad time intervals drops the
  profile-shape error from 14.2% at K=1 to 10.5% at K=3 and 9.7% at K=8. Most
  of the reachable improvement is at a time resolution a shared law could
  express. `figures/stage34_temporal_gompertz_log-E2-S2.png`.
- **Where**: the efficiency law lives in `model.py::log_eps`; add a nested
  option so that setting the new coefficients to zero reproduces the current
  model exactly, and assert that.
- **How to judge it**: the existing seven-criterion panel. Score on the
  **per-epoch completeness-controlled mask**, not the full sample.
- **The trap to avoid**: do **not** measure success against the 4.5% bound.
  Fitting one galaxy's deposits to a *different* galaxy's profile already
  reaches 5.0%, so roughly seven eighths of the 14.2%-to-4.5% gap is the
  flexibility of the deposit set, not information a shared law can use. The
  honest target is the wrong-galaxy number, and anything approaching it should
  be treated as suspicious rather than successful.
- **What would count as success**: the shape error moving toward 10–11% without
  the halo-mass bias reopening (currently +0.003 to +0.012 dex per dex) and
  without the parameters becoming undetermined.
- **Prior**: a *free flexible curve* in redshift was already tried and was the
  worst of the 45 variants, with essentially undetermined parameters. So keep
  the new form **low-order and nested**, and expect the identifiability report
  to be the binding constraint rather than the loss.

## Problems / Blockers

- **The model still loses to a plain statistical fit at total stellar mass**,
  by ~36% in scatter at redshift 0.7 and above on the completeness-controlled
  sample (0.15–0.22 dex). Unexplained. This is next-step 3 and the deployment
  case.
- **The loss we fit is blind in two places**: at redshift 2 only 6.3% of the
  stellar mass lies beyond 52 kpc, so an error below 1% on the cumulative curve
  is a 30% error in that annulus; and once each epoch is renormalised the loss
  barely feels the never-decreasing constraint. This is next-step 2, and it may
  well need doing before step 1 shows its full value.
- **The identifiability claim is incomplete** until genuine profile likelihoods
  are run (fix one parameter, re-optimise the other six, repeat on a grid).

## Key Decisions

- **Report the bound on a fixed cohort followed at all five epochs**, not on a
  per-epoch mask. The masked solve admits 542 of 2397 galaxies at one epoch
  only, where 24 radii against ~71 free weights bound nothing.
- **Keep "mh-complete" as the sample name** (user's call) but define it in place
  every time: above the halo mass where completeness first reaches 60% of its
  redshift-0.4 maximum of 0.781, i.e. about 47% of all haloes of that mass.
- **Deprioritise rather than retire the compact second component.** It cannot
  address a central decline, which is the larger half of the redshift-2
  deficit, but it remains reasonable for the non-declining galaxies.
- **A redshift-dependent deposit shape stays rejected**, now confirmed on all
  2397 galaxies and on the 840 followed.
- **Preserve version 1's output** at
  `outputs/stage34_ceiling_v1_provisional.npz` rather than overwriting it.
