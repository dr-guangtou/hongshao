### exp52 BUG FIX (affects a number already in the record)

`decompose.py` divided the model's APERTURE growth by the truth's ANNULUS
growth: the truth term was `interp(r_out) - interp(r_in)` with `r_in = 0.0`,
and numpy's `interp` CLAMPS below the grid, returning the CoG at 2 kpc rather
than zero, while the model term used the analytic `enclosed(0.0)=0`. The
truth's `M(<2 kpc)` FALLS over z=1.0->0.4 (-6.55e12 Msun, 62% of galaxies
negative), inflating the denominator by 27%.

**`M(<10)` model/truth growth is 0.726, not 0.573.** Finding survives at 27%
rather than 43%. Transport shares are UNAFFECTED (no truth denominator), so
exp52's mechanism headline stands. Fixed, README annotated, corrected
decomposition re-run and confirmed (0.727), assertions added to `score.py`.
