## Critical warnings (all measured this session)

- **SERIAL by default.** The pooled fit path hangs after a few fits in one
  process: 0% CPU, no live workers, parent blocked in `pool.map`, workers
  reporting BrokenPipeError on multiprocessing's ERROR path. It cost hours
  twice. `objective.fit` and `shootout.fit` are serial with `--pool` /
  `use_pool=True` to opt back in.
- **Monitor with a CPU-based STALL ALARM, not just completion.** A hung
  job and a slow job look identical; the alarm caught the third
  occurrence in 15 minutes.
- **scipy Nelder-Mead freezes zero-initialized parameters**
  (`zdelt = 2.5e-4`). Any parameter that nests an incumbent at 0 needs an
  explicit `initial_simplex`. A false null was produced this way.
- **Loss and observables can be ANTI-CORRELATED** — rank candidates with
  `exp47/judge.py`, never by loss.
- Long serial fits run 5 min to ~3 h each; stores save after EVERY fit and
  skip cached cells, so all runs are resumable.
