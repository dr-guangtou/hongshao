### What was established (all asserted in `--selftest`)

1. The stored five-epoch CoGs are the **`xy` sky projection** — z=0.4 apertures
   bit-identical to the `xy` table rows for 3304/3388 galaxies, no other
   projection.
2. They use a **CONSTANT isophotal shape**, `R` = semi-major axis: reproduces to
   **0.0027 dex** against 0.038 for the radius-by-radius ellipticity profile and
   0.18 for a circular aperture.
3. That shape is recorded only at z=0.4 but **recoverable at every epoch two
   independent ways** — CoG/circular ratio (corr **0.995**) and a **5–30 kpc
   unweighted mean** of the ellipticity profile (corr **0.985**). Their
   difference is a certificate needing no recorded reference.
4. `Cov(M_i,M_j) = Var(M_min(i,j))`: the 24 CoG radii have **effective rank
   1.22**; the annulus form (`M(<2kpc)` + 23 annuli) is exactly invertible and
   **diagonalises** the covariance (rank 7.75).
5. `ndata` sits at photutils' floor of 13 out to **2.41 kpc** — the inner
   isophotes are not independently resolved.

### The error model, tested by fitting with it (§11)

GLS fit of a flexible 5-parameter family, 800 galaxies, 19 dof. Median reduced χ²:

| covariance | z=0.4 | z=0.7 | z=1.0 | z=1.5 | z=2.0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sigma_iso`, diagonal | 0.4 | 0.3 | 0.2 | 0.1 | 0.03 |
| `sigma_iso`, full covariance | 3.4 | 3.4 | 3.7 | 4.6 | 7.6 |
| `+ sigma_shape` | 3.4 | 3.4 | 3.7 | 4.6 | 7.6 |
| `+ sigma_proj` (z=0.4 only) | **1.1** | — | — | — | — |

- The **measured** projection term completes the error model at z=0.4.
- The **diagonal flatters the fit by 10–200×**.
- The coherent shape term buys **nothing** — a cumulative covariance already
  makes a common mode cheap.
- Density cross-check: pull of (CoG rebuilt from density − stored) / predicted σ
  has width 1.15 at 2 kpc, 0.39–0.69 outside, at every epoch.

## Key Decisions

Eight design decisions were settled with the user in an interview (§10.1):

- **`cog_provided` remains the fitting target.** Nothing replaces it.
- The **isophote `intensity` is the fiducial density** — *within the density
  product*; this is not a change of fitting target.
- **One shared 32-radius grid** (0.5608–159.74 kpc, ratio 1.2) — the dominant
  *native* ladder, so 75.6% of galaxy-epochs store the measurement itself with no
  interpolation.
- **Density exactly zero past each galaxy's cutoff**, which makes the
  reconstructed CoG flat *by construction*. Costs ≤0.010 dex at 148 kpc, and
  truncated and untruncated galaxies are indistinguishable (−0.010 vs −0.009 at
  z=2), so the residual is the smooth-model bias, not the truncation.
- **Semi-major axis primary**, `r_circ` alongside (the two differ by a 6.1% epoch
  differential).
- **Inner isophotes kept, flagged `sigma_resolved`, errors untouched** — the
  product records what was measured; weighting is the likelihood's decision.
- **Projection term raw only**, no extrapolation in the file.
- **Negative annuli kept and flagged, not repaired** (0.8% of z=2 annuli) — the
  transform must stay exactly invertible.
- **Versioning: v2 cut, v1 kept readable.** v2 is a strict superset.

### Two corrections made during the session

- **`test` does not gate the ellipticity profile.** It gates the isophote fit's
  *convergence*. 35% of `test=False` galaxy-epochs carry full, non-degenerate
  ellipticity/PA profiles. Correct coverage on the fitting sample is **1517** at
  all five epochs, not the 1011 first quoted. The right gate is `has_shape_cols`.
- **Projection is the dominant *measurement* error and only ~3% of the *signal*.**
  It is 2.5× the statistical term at 10 kpc and completes the z=0.4 error model,
  *and* it is at most **3.2%** of the variance at fixed halo mass (0.2% at
  150 kpc). An earlier claim in §6 implied it could excuse population-level model
  failure; §11.3 corrects that explicitly. Quote both numbers together.
