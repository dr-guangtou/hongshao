## Problems / Blockers — read these before deciding anything

### P-A. The transport comparison is confounded by the amplitude pin. SETTLE THIS FIRST.

The user's "systematically falling behind" is the claim that most affects what
happens next, and it is **not yet a like-for-like comparison**. exp54's own
re-baselining (`rebaseline.py`, README Stage 2) measured:

| tier 3 max\|rel\| beyond 5 kpc | z=0.4 | z=2.0 |
|---|---|---|
| transport kernel, **pinned** to measured `M*(<500 kpc)` | 0.1951 | 0.1648 |
| the SAME shape, amplitude **predicted** | 0.2690 | 0.3150 |
| exp54 incumbent (deposition-only) | 0.2765 | 0.3233 |
| exp57 `X3` small-core | 0.2765 | **0.3178** |

The apparent deficit is 0.195 → 0.277 at z=0.4, but **0.269 of it is the pin**.
Like for like the gap is **+2.8%** at z=0.4 and **+0.9%** at z=2.

**Two caveats that must be checked, not assumed**: the re-baselining numbers
**predate the row-181 fix** and may be contaminated; and the two runs may not be
on identical galaxy sets. **There is no stored `qa.evaluate` output from the
transport-kernel era** — I searched. So this has never actually been measured
head to head.

### P-C. The standard QA battery cannot see what exp57 changed.

Tier 3 max|rel| beyond 5 kpc is **0.2765 for the incumbent and 0.2765 for `X3`

## Key Decisions

- **Bounding the expansion FACTOR was the wrong design** and the experiment's
  main technical finding. A factor bounded at 2.47× still threw 85% of moved
  mass past 50 kpc, because a power-law tail has most of its outer mass at large
  radius. If a gate is stated in kpc, bound the mechanism in kpc.
- **`X3` does NOT nest `X1`.** Two drafts claimed it did; the self-check
  falsified both. They are different mass-conservation conventions — `X1`
  carries a deposit's truncation outward with its stars, `X3` holds it at the
  halo's `3 R200c`. Both conserve mass exactly within their own boundary.
- **`X4` rejected despite the best loss**, because its conditioning slope is
  unidentified (three signs across ten starts) and its winning sign contradicts
  the pre-registered prediction. The formation-time signal (`r = −0.317`) is
  real; this parameterisation of it is not.
- **Nothing adopted.** exp54's `gompertz_log-E2-S2` still stands at 13.787%.
