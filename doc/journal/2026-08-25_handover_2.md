## Exp55 Result That Exp56 Inherits

- The selected analytic profile is a compact Sérsic component plus an extended
  Moffat component:

  ```text
  M*(<R) = M*,148 [
      f_compact F_Sersic(R; R_compact, n) / F_Sersic(148; ...)
      + (1-f_compact) F_Moffat(R; R_extended, gamma)
                               / F_Moffat(148; ...)
  ].
  ```

- The four galaxy-level coordinates are:
  - `log_mstar_148`: stellar mass inside 148.2 kpc.
  - `compact_fraction_148`: compact component fraction inside 148.2 kpc.
  - `log_r_compact`: compact-component scale radius.
  - `log_r_extended`: extended-component scale radius, constrained above the
    compact radius.
- These are phenomenological profile coordinates. No in-situ/ex-situ
  interpretation has been established.
- The original global grid tested only `n = 1, 2, 4`; `n=1` was selected
  in all five folds and is therefore at the lower tested boundary.
- At fixed `n=1, gamma=1.25`, the analytic representation reached:
  - 0.00437 dex median CoG RMS over 2.0--148.2 kpc.
  - 0.06601 dex median density-profile RMS.
  - Median scaled-Jacobian smallest-to-largest singular-value ratio
    `10^-1.70`.
  - 2.55% of galaxies with any coordinate within 1% of a bound.
- The held-out relation from four DiffMAH parameters plus `c200c` explained:
  - 86.5% of variance in stellar mass inside 148.2 kpc.
  - 33.7% in extended radius.
  - 6.0% in compact fraction.
  - 6.8% in compact radius.
- The complete relation improved profile CRPS by:
  - 25.84% relative to final halo mass alone.
  - 13.20% relative to mass-conditioned shuffled MAH.
  - 4.44% relative to mass-conditioned shuffled concentration.
- The original fixed outer Moffat slope `gamma=1.25` changed the TNG
  stellar-mass--R90 slope from 0.291 to 0.251 before any halo prediction,
  despite its 0.00437 dex median CoG RMS.
- Fixed `gamma=1.4` is the preferred single-slope baseline:
  - Density RMS improved from 0.06601 to 0.06093 dex.
  - Generated R90 energy-distance ratio improved from 3.40 to 2.20, where
    one is the TNG split-half sampling floor.
- A hard mass-dependent boundary using `gamma=1.4` below each training
  fold's two-thirds halo-mass quantile and `gamma=1.25` above it passed every
  declared gate:
  - Profile CRPS: 0.06377 dex.
  - Nominal-68% coverage: 68.9%.
  - R90 energy-distance ratio: 0.96.
  - Largest held-out mass-bin shape residual: 2.74%.
- The smooth candidate used
  `gamma=1.25 + 0.15/[1+exp((logMpeak-transition)/width)]`.
  All five folds selected width 0.20 dex from the extended
  0.05--0.80 dex grid.
- The smooth model recovered the intended outer-size relation:
  - Profile CRPS: 0.06391 dex.
  - Nominal-68% coverage: 69.5%.
  - Median R90 energy ratio across 32 populations: 0.92.
  - Median stellar-mass--R90 slope: 0.2889 versus 0.2909 in TNG.
- The smooth law was not formally adopted:
  - Its self-consistent size-scaled energy ratio was 0.72075, 10.0034% above
    the hard boundary's 0.65521 and just outside the declared 10% allowance.
  - Its largest coherent mass-bin shape residual was 3.34%, worse than the
    hard boundary's 2.74%.
  - The remaining coherent residual is concentrated mainly at 5--30 kpc.
- Actual-minus-DiffMAH history residuals correlated only -0.030 to +0.045
  with the R90 error and did not beat mass-conditioned shuffles. Exp56 must
  not assume that DiffMAH compression caused the remaining profile residual.
